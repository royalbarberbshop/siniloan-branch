Royal Barber Shop customer payment options phase

This patch introduces 50% downpayment and 100% full payment choices on the customer page, QR display, screenshot/reference submission, private proof storage, and database-side administrator-only payment approval.

IMPORTANT: This is a REVIEW BUILD, not a production-complete payment workflow. The admin approval screen, POS balance-only collection, and rescheduling-with-payment-retention are not yet implemented. Read docs/CUSTOMER_PAYMENT_OPTIONS_PHASE.md before applying the migration. Apply to DEMO first. Do not deploy to LIVE until the remaining integration work is completed and tested.
